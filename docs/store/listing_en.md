## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
The easiest way to track water — done with one widget tap

💧 See it at a glance on your home screen widget, log with one tap
📱 Log instantly, one tap and done
🔒 3 widget styles (mini, card, wide) — lock screen placement available on supported devices

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

■ Log without ever opening the app
One tap on the widget and that's it. No launching the app, hunting for a screen, or tapping
buttons — just build the habit of drinking water.

■ More than just water — coffee, tea, juice, and milk too
Log everything you drink throughout the day, not just water. Each drink type gets its own icon,
so you can see what you had at a glance.

■ Build a habit with your streak
Every day you hit your goal, your streak grows. Small wins add up into a real habit, and a
celebration animation cheers you on each time you get there.

■ Reminders that fit you
Set your own reminder interval (30 minutes to 4 hours) and time window. No reminders on days
you've already hit your goal.

■ Full dark mode support
The app and every widget automatically follow your system's dark mode setting.

■ Stats at a glance
See your last 7 days in a graph, along with your weekly average and how many days you hit your
goal.

■ Every feature, free
Streak protection, smart stats (30-day trend, annual heatmap), CSV export, widget color
themes — no payment required for any of it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

water reminder, hydration tracker, drink water reminder, home screen widget, water widget,
8 glasses a day, healthy habit, water tracker, drink water app, hydration widget
```
